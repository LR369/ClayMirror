 Clay Mirror

A potter's wheel for Snap Specs. Place a real-sized workbench in your space, drop a block of clay on the spinning wheel, and shape it with your hand to match the target piece. Pinch Done to see your score on the inspector's board, collect XP, wipe the wheel with the sponge, and move on through 10 pieces (Mug to Urn) to earn your potter's certificate.

## Open it

1. Open `clay_mirror.esproj` in Lens Studio (5.22 or later).
2. In the Preview panel, use Interactive Preview: WASD to move, right-drag to look around, click to pinch.
3. Look down at the bench, drag it and set its height, then pinch Start.

## What's inside

- `Assets/Scripts/`: the game (TypeScript, Spectacles Interaction Kit + UI Kit)
- `Assets/Tests/`: 16 LEAF test scenarios, one per done criterion (run them from the LEAF panel)
- `Assets/GeneratedSFX/`: sounds and music
- `devpost/design-brief.md`: the design brief this was built from

Logic is proven in the Lens Studio preview; the feel of sculpting with real hand tracking is still to be tried on Specs.
